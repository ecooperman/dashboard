Requests

People who signed in and asked for an app. Approving lets them in (one email) and works exactly like ticking their box below; whatever you leave unticked is turned down, and they can't ask for it again.

Add someone

Lets them into the apps you tick, and emails them once. You can change the ticks any time below. The name is optional, only so you can tell people apart.

Who can use what

Ticking gives access, unticking takes it away (nothing of theirs is deleted, and ticking again puts it all back). Administrators always keep access. Permanently deleting a removed person's data is still done in that app's own Users page.
